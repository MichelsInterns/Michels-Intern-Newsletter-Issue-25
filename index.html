Today is all about celebrating you! From job sites and offices to projects across the country, thank you for making an impact this summer. We hope you enjoy this special edition featuring your stories, photos, and highlights.
SUMMER THROUGH YOUR EYES
One of the best parts of this internship is seeing the many places, experiences, and memories that make each summer unique. Whether you’re exploring a new city, spending time outdoors, attending a local event, or enjoying a weekend with friends and family, every photo tells a story. 

Here’s a look at some of the moments our interns have shared throughout the summer. Thank you for letting us be part of your journey; we love seeing where your Michels adventure takes you! 

Keep making memories, taking photos, and enjoying everything this summer has to offer. ☀️
